A payment event is not yet an enterprise control.

Stablecoin settlement must preserve the immutable chain fact while keeping order references, accounting authority, manufacturing evidence and human review distinct. ArcPaymentReceipt is the D09 payment component—not the umbrella for the enterprise portfolio.

04 — The control path

Fact → evidence → human decision.

1. Chain factArc Testnet anchors a payment or manufacturing state without silently rewriting history.
2. Read-only evidenceRender presents source-separated receipts and checks without a signer, credential or write endpoint.
3. Human gateAny future ERP, Circle or wallet action stays action-time approved and independently evidenced.
No public wallet connection, ERP write, Circle subscription, webhook receiver or secret is enabled by this product surface.
